Live Wire

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

What this app collects

Live Wire has no account system and no sign-in. You are never asked for your name, email, or any identifying information to play.

If you choose to submit a score to the public leaderboard, or play Live Race, the app sends three things to our database (hosted by Supabase):

  • A display name — whatever you type in, entirely your choice. Don't use your real name if you'd rather not.
  • Your score and level reached in that session.
  • During a live race only, a randomly generated device identifier is used momentarily to show your race progress to other players currently racing — it is not stored permanently and is not tied to you personally.

That's the complete list. Nothing else leaves your device.

Public by design

Scores and display names on the leaderboard, and progress shown during a live race, are visible to anyone else playing Live Wire — that's the point of a shared leaderboard and real-time race. Don't submit a score under a name you wouldn't want other players to see.

What it doesn't do

  • No account creation, login, email, or password, ever
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs, and no ad network
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone
  • No data sold or shared with anyone beyond what's described above

Local storage

Your campaign progress, unlocked customization, and settings are saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. It is never sent anywhere.

Third-party processor

Leaderboard scores and live race data are stored using Supabase, a hosted database provider. They process this data on our behalf to make the leaderboard and live race features work, and don't use it for any purpose of their own.

Children

Live Wire does not knowingly collect any information that identifies a real person, from anyone, including children. The optional display name is a free-text nickname, not a real identity.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes in a future update, this page will be updated and the date above will change accordingly.
