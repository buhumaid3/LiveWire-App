Support & Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

What's the goal?
Drag the nodes to untangle the wire so no lines cross, before the power gauge runs out. Clear it faster for a bigger speed bonus.
What are Hint, Undo, Snip, and Bomb?
They're boosters you can use mid-level: Hint shows a helpful move, Undo reverses your last drag, Snip and Bomb clear a tricky crossing or node for you. Snip and Bomb cost Sparks, the in-game currency you earn by playing.
What are zones and boss levels?
Every 10 levels you enter a new zone with its own look — the Garage, Night Circuit, Blueprint Lab, Server Farm, and the Power Grid. Every 10th level is a boss level with extra hazards; clearing one unlocks that zone's schematic collectible.

Daily Challenge & Live Race

What's the Daily Challenge?
One puzzle, the same for every player, resets every day. Your fastest clear time is tracked as your personal best.
How does Live Race work?
It's a real-time 10-level sprint against whoever else is racing at the same moment. First to clear level 10 wins — you can see opponents' progress update live as you both play.
Is the leaderboard real?
Yes — scores are shared across every player of Live Wire, split into All-Time and This Week.

Troubleshooting

My progress didn't save / looks reset.
Progress is stored on your device. Reinstalling the app, or switching to a new device without transferring data, will start you fresh. Leaderboard scores you've already submitted are not affected.
I found a bug, or something looks wrong.
Please reach out below with your device model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened — that's the fastest way to get it fixed.
